## The headline is an artifact. Do not act on it.

A screen for "protocols down >20% this week" flags Convex at **−21.5%**. That is arithmetically
correct and analytically wrong.

| window | change | from → to |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 7 days | **−21.5%** | 2026-08-11 $615.4M → $483.0M |
| 30 days | **−2.3%** | 2026-07-19 $494.3M → $483.0M |
| 90 days | −17.9% | 2026-05-20 $588.3M → $483.0M |
| 365 days | **−67.8%** | 2025-08-18 $1,498.1M → $483.0M |

The seven-day window **begins on a one-day spike**. The largest single-day move in the past
month is **+27.5% on 2026-08-11**, which lifted TVL to $615.4M for a single day before it
reverted to the $485M level it had held all month.

So the week did not see a $132M outflow. It saw a one-day inflow that left. Measured over
thirty days, Convex is **flat to slightly down (−2.3%)**.

**The actionable point:** any risk screen keyed on 7-day TVL change will misclassify this
protocol, and will misclassify every protocol whose window happens to open on a spike. Check the
single-day series before treating a weekly number as a trend.

## The real risk is the one the weekly number hides

Convex is **down 67.8% over twelve months** and **97.7% from its peak** of $21.17B on
2022-01-05. That is the finding worth having. It is a protocol operating at roughly one
forty-fourth of its high-water mark, on a slow multi-year decline rather than an acute event.

## Audits — and a warning about the field you would use to check

DefiLlama records `audits: "2"` for Convex, with a published audit link:
`github.com/convex-eth/platform/blob/main/audit/Convex Platform Security Audit Report.pdf`

**Do not use that field as a risk signal for other protocols.** `audits: "0"` means *DefiLlama
holds no audit link*, not *the protocol is unaudited*. Protocols currently carrying `"0"`
include **BlackRock BUIDL ($3.5B), Circle USYC ($3.0B), Coinbase Bridge ($6.3B) and Arbitrum
Bridge ($2.7B)** — institutions that are certainly audited. LayerZero V2 ($6.7B) has the field
missing entirely.

Any due-diligence pipeline that scores "unaudited" from this field will produce false negatives
on the largest and most institutional protocols in the dataset. It is a data-coverage flag, and
this report treats it as one.

## What I cannot verify, stated rather than bluffed

- **Team assessment.** I have no way to verify identities, employment or track record from
  public on-chain data. Any report claiming to have done so from these sources is guessing.
- **Audit quality.** A linked PDF is evidence an audit happened, not evidence it was good or
  current.
- **Governance intent** and treasury policy. Not derivable from TVL.
- **Whether the 08-11 spike was organic.** I can show it happened and reverted. I cannot show
  who did it or why from this data.

Most due-diligence reports assert all four. Ask for the source next time you read one.

## Method and reproduction

```bash
node report.mjs convex-finance   # writes figures.json from public APIs
node verify.mjs                  # re-derives every number in this file from that JSON
```

**Two traps this cost me, both worth knowing if you do this yourself:**

1. **The TVL series contains duplicate dates.** There were 2 rows dated 2026-08-18. Indexing
   "seven days ago" as `array[len-8]` therefore lands on the wrong day and returns **−2.5%**
   instead of −21.5% — a tenfold error, in the direction that makes a moving protocol look
   calm. Everything here is indexed by date, and the duplicate count is reported in
   `figures.json`.
2. **Two DefiLlama endpoints can disagree.** The generator cross-checks its own 7-day figure
   against the `change_7d` the protocols list reports, and records whether they agree. They do,
   to within a point — after the indexing was fixed. Before it, they differed by 19 points and
   the mismatch is what exposed the bug.
